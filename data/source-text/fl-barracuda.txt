 Collier through Martin counties
 Slot Limit: 15-36" fork length with 1 fish greater than 36" per person or vessel per day
Daily Bag Limit: 2 fish per person, 6 per vessel
Season: Open year-round
Special regulations apply for this species when fishing in Biscayne National Park.
Includes all waters off Collier, Monroe, Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, and Martin counties.
 Rest of state
 Minimum Size Limit: None
Daily Bag Limit: 2 fish or 100 pounds, whichever is more
Season: Open year-round
