FF-30-2022
PROCLAMATION
RE: DOLPHIN– RECREATIONAL VESSEL LIMIT – COASTAL FISHING WATERS
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-27-2012 dated April 10, 2012. This
proclamation reduces the recreational vessel limit for dolphin in N.C. Coastal Fishing Waters
in compliance with the South Atlantic Fishery Management Council’s Dolphin and Wahoo
Fishery Management Plan.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01 A.M.,
Monday, May 2, 2022, the following restrictions will apply to the dolphin fishery in Coastal Fishing
Waters:
I. SUSPENSION OF N.C. MARINE FISHERIES COMMISSION RULE 15A NCAC 03M .0515
The following portion of N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03M. 0515 that
reads as follows is suspended:
Section (a)(2) which reads:
(2) More than 60 dolphin per vessel per day regardless of the number of individuals on
board, except headboat vessels with a valid U.S. Coast Guard Certificate of Inspection
may possess 10 dolphin per paying customer.
II. RECREATIONAL VESSEL LIMIT
It is unlawful to possess more than 54 dolphin per vessel per day, except headboat vessels
with a valid U.S. Coast Guard Certificate of Inspection may possess 10 dolphin per paying
customer.
III. RESTRICTION ON SALE
It is unlawful to sell or offer for sale dolphin taken or possessed under the recreational bag limit
by a person on a vessel operating as a charter vessel or headboat.
IV. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C. G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine
Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. The remaining portions of N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03M.
0515 remain in effect.
D. The intent of this proclamation is to manage the recreational fishery for dolphin in
Coastal Fishing Waters consistent with the South Atlantic Fishery Management
Council’s Dolphin and Wahoo Fishery Management Plan Amendment 10. The
Proclamation FF-30-2022
Pg. 2
amendment and the rules implementing the regulations can be found here:
