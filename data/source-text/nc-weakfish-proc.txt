FF-10-2024
PROCLAMATION
RE: WEAKFISH – RECREATIONAL – COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING WATERS
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-54-2010(REVISED) dated May 21, 2010.
It maintains the recreational size and creel limits for the weakfish fishery in N.C.
Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters in accordance with the Atlantic States Marine
Fisheries Commission Addendum IV to Amendment 4 to the Weakfish Fishery
Management Plan.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at
12:01 A.M., January 1, 2024, the following restrictions will apply to the recreational
weakfish fisheries in North Carolina Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters:
I. SIZE AND CREEL LIMITS
A. It is unlawful to possess weakfish for recreational purposes less than 12 inches in
total length.
B. It is unlawful to possess for recreational purposes no more than one (1) weakfish
per person per day or per person per trip, whichever is more restrictive.
II. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1;
113-170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52; and N.C.
Marine Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Director
under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule
15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. The intent of this proclamation is to maintain compliance with the Atlantic States
Marine Fisheries Commission Addendum IV to Amendment 4 to the Weakfish
Fishery Management Plan.
D. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c), all persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep
themselves informed of current proclamations.
E. Contact the N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC
28557, phone 252-726-7021 or 800-682-2632 for more information or visit the
division website at https://deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries.
F. This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-54-2010(REVISED) dated May
10, 2010. It maintains the recreational size and creel limits for the weakfish
