SF-4-2024
PROCLAMATION
RE: CALICO SCALLOP – ATLANTIC OCEAN - CLOSURE
This proclamation supersedes proclamation SC-3-2001(REVISED) dated February 14, 2001,
and closes the harvest calico scallops in the Atlantic Ocean.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01
A.M., May 31, 2024, the following restrictions shall apply to harvest of calico scallops in the
Atlantic Ocean:
I. HARVEST RESTRICTIONS:
It shall be unlawful to harvest calico scallops from North Carolina in the Atlantic Ocean from
0 to 3 miles as defined in G.S. 141-6(a).
II. GENERAL INFORMATION:
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-184; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52; and N.C. Marine
Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03K .0504.
B. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103
it is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority.
C. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c) all persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep
themselves informed of current proclamations.
D. The intent of this proclamation is to close harvest of calico scallops from North
Carolina water in the Atlantic Ocean due to lack of reported landings from the
fishery.
E. Contact N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC
28557; 252-515-5500 or 800-682-2632 for more information or visit the division
website at www.ncmarinefisheries.net.
F. This proclamation closes the harvest calico scallops in the Atlantic Ocean.
