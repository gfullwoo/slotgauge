FF-1-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE: BLUEFISH – RECREATIONAL – COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING WATERS
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-31-2022, dated April 28, 2022. This
proclamation increases the private and For-Hire harvest limits for the recreational
bluefish fishery.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at
12:01 A.M., Thursday, January 1, 2026, the following restrictions will apply to the
recreational bluefish fishery in Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters:
I. PRIVATE HARVEST LIMIT (All persons not engaged on a For-Hire vessel)
It shall be unlawful to possess more than five (5) bluefish per person per day taken
for recreational purposes.
II. FOR-HIRE HARVEST LIMIT (While engaged on a For-Hire vessel as defined by
G.S. 113-174)
It shall be unlawful to possess more than seven (7) bluefish per person per day
taken in a for hire operation.
III. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1;
113-170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52; and N.C.
Marine Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Director
under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission
Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. The intent of this proclamation is to allow North Carolina to comply with the
requirements of the Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council/Atlantic States
Marine Fisheries Commission Bluefish Fishery Management Plan
D. It shall be unlawful under N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC
03O .0112 to operate a For-Hire vessel unless the vessel operator possesses
the For-Hire Blanket Coastal Recreational Fishing License (CRFL) for the vessel.
E. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c) all persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to
keep themselves informed of current proclamations.
