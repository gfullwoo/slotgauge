 Size, Season & Creel Limits
 Last Updated: September 23, 2026
Topic
Previous (2025)
New (2026)
Lunker Bass Lakes – Round Valley & Merrill Creek Reservoirs 
Both reservoirs followed the statewide bass rule: 12-inch minimum, 5-fish combined daily limit. 
Both reservoirs added to the Lunker Bass Lake list: minimum size increased to 15 inches, daily limit reduced to 3 fish combined. 
Ryker Lake – Bass & Panfish Rules 
Special Conservation Regulations applied: 15-inch minimum and 2-fish combined limit for Smallmouth Bass, plus special sunfish and Yellow Perch rules. 
Conservation Regulations removed; Ryker Lake now follows the general statewide rule (12-inch minimum, 5-fish combined limit), plus general sunfish and Yellow Perch rules. 
 Trout Stocked rivers and streams are closed to fishing for all species from March 23 to April 11, 2026, at 8 a.m. because of trout stocking. Refer to General Trout Regulations for complete lists. Regulations in red are new this year. See Delaware River and Greenwood Lake regulations.
