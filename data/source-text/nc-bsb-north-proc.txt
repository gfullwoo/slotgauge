PROCLAMATION
RE: BLACK SEA BASS – RECREATIONAL – NORTH OF CAPE HATTERAS – 2026
RECREATIONAL SEASON, SIZE, AND CREEL LIMITS
This proclamation supersedes Proclamation FF-20-2025, dated April 25, 2025. This
proclamation establishes the 2026 season dates, size, and creel limits for the recreational black
sea bass fishery north of Cape Hatteras effective at 12:01 A.M., May 1, 2026. The fishery will
close at 11:59 P.M., December 31, 2026.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01 A.M.,
May 1, 2026, the following restrictions will apply to the recreational black sea bass fishery in Coastal
Fishing Waters which includes the Atlantic Ocean north of Cape Hatteras (35° 15.3’N. Latitude):
I. SEASON
It shall be unlawful to possess black sea bass for recreational purposes except from 12:01 A.M.,
May 1, 2026, through 11:59 P.M., December 31, 2026.
II. SIZE AND CREEL LIMIT
A. It shall be unlawful to possess black sea bass less than 13 inches total length (excluding
the caudal fin filament).
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than 15 black sea bass per person per day taken for
recreational purposes.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess black sea bass for recreational purposes from Coastal
Fishing Waters which includes the Atlantic Ocean north of Cape Hatteras except during
the seasons specified in Section I.
III. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine
Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries Director
under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A
NCAC 03H .0103.
C. See https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries/rules-proclamations-and-
size-and-bag-limits/fisheries-management-proclamations#currentprocs to refer to those
Snapper Grouper proclamations for regulations for the recreational black sea bass fishery
south of Cape Hatteras.
D. The intent of this proclamation is to maintain North Carolina’s compliance with the
requirements of the joint Mid-Atlantic Fishery Management Council/Atlantic States Marine
Fisheries Commission Summer Flounder, Scup, and Black Sea Bass Fishery
Management Plan.
