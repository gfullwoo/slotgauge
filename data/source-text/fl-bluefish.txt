 Bluefish
 Regulation Update
 Effective Oct. 26, 2020:
The recreational daily bag limit is three fish per person along the Atlantic coast from Nassau through Miami-Dade.
Why: A 2019 federal stock assessment found Atlantic bluefish is overfished. Federal fishery managers adopted more restrictive recreational bag limits in federal waters, and the Atlantic States Marine Fisheries Management Commission requested states implement similar regulations.
 Atlantic state waters
 Minimum size limit: 12" fork length
Daily bag limit: 3 fish per person from Nassau through Miami-Dade counties
 Atlantic federal waters
 (regulated by the Mid-Atlantic Fisheries Management Council)
Minimum size limit: none
Daily bag limit: 3 per person for anglers fishing from private recreational vessels and 5 per person on for-hire vessels
For-hire vessels are required to have a federal bluefish charter permit to keep bluefish. 
 Gulf state and federal waters
 Minimum Size Limit: 12" fork length
Daily Bag Limit: 10 per harvester (includes Monroe County)
