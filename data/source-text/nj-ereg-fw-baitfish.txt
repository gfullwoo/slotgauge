American Eel – Bait Collection Limit 
Eels counted toward the 35-per-day combined baitfish limit with no separate cap. 
Combined baitfish limit stays 35 per day, but no more than 25 of those may be American Eel. 
Snapping Turtle – Season 
Season ran April 1 to May 14 and July 1 to Oct. 31. 
Spring window removed; season is now limited to July 15 to Oct. 31 only. 
Snapping Turtle – Minimum Size 
12-inch minimum carapace length. 
Minimum size increased to 13 inches, measured along the curved carapace. 
Snapping Turtle – Possession Restriction 
No consumption-only restriction stated. 
New for 2026: snapping turtles may now be taken for consumption only.

 See separate regulations for Delaware River. 
 For marine waters, see the New Jersey Marine Digest.
 Baitfish Regulations
 Baitfish Species
 Only the following species may be taken for use as bait. American Eel, Banded Killifish, Creek Chub, Fallfish, Fathead Minnow, Gizzard Shad, Golden Shiner, Margined Madtom, Mummichog, and Tadpole Madtom. For Herring, Alewife and Blueback—see below.

 Gear
 Gear Description
 Seine
 Not over 50 feet long in ponds and lakes over 100 acres; in all other waters not over 30 feet in length. 
Exception: In trout-stocked waters and special regulation trout areas a seine may not be more than 10 feet in length and 4 feet in depth.
 Minnow Trap
 Not larger than 24 inches in length with a funnel mouth no greater than 2 inches in diameter.
 Umbrella Net
 No greater than 3.5 feet square.
 Dip Net
 Not more than 24 inches in diameter; may be used only for alewife or blueback herring.
 Cast Net
 No greater than 8 feet in diameter; may be used only in streams that are not trout-stocked or special regulation trout areas (may not be used in lakes).
 Hook and Line
 Maximum of nine single hooks or three hooks with three burrs per contrivance on all waters except the Delaware River where only three single hooks are permitted.

 Means of Take
 Snapping turtles may only be taken in fresh waters of the state and for consumption only. See page 30 for established fishing license boundary lines that designate between fresh and marine waters. Any person with a valid fishing license or those entitled to fish without a license may take snapping turtles, bull frogs, and green frogs by means of spears, traps, hook and line, dip nets (not more than 24 inches in diameter), or by hand. They may NOT be sold unless taken under a Commercial Harvest Permit. Bull frogs, and green frogs may be taken in numbers greater than the daily limit under a Commercial Harvest Permit issued by NJDEP Fish & Wildlife at its discretion. The commercial harvest of snapping turtles is closed to new harvesters. Existing harvesters may contact Eric Boehm for application information.
