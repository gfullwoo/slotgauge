 Regulations for Warmwater Game Fish
 State waters are classified as: inland, joint and coastal (see “Gen­eral Information”). These state waters are collectively referred to as public fishing waters. Certain fishes, including large­mouth bass, crappie and mountain trout are designated inland game fish and under the jurisdiction of the Wildlife Resources Com­­mission in all public waters; whereas, other species, including striped bass, white and yellow perch, flounder and red drum, are desig­nated as inland game fish only in inland waters.
