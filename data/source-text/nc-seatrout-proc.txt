FF-31-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE: SPOTTED SEATROUT – COMMERCIAL AND RECREATIONAL – COASTAL AND
JOINT FISHING WATERS INCLUDING THE ATLANTIC OCEAN - OPENING AND
HARVEST RESTRICTIONS
This proclamation supersedes proclamations FF-12-2026, dated February 4, 2026, and FF-
37-2025, dated September 29, 2025. This proclamation opens the commercial and
recreational spotted seatrout (speckled trout) fishery in Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters
including the Atlantic Ocean on July 1, 2026, in accordance with Amendment 1 to the N.C.
Spotted Seatrout Fishery Management Plan.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective 12:01
A.M., July 1, 2026, the following restrictions will apply to spotted seatrout in Coastal and Joint
Fishing Waters including the Atlantic Ocean:
I. RECREATIONAL SIZE AND HARVEST RESTRICTIONS
A. It shall be unlawful to possess spotted seatrout taken for recreational purposes less
than 14 inches total length or greater than 20 inches total length except that one (1)
spotted seatrout over 26 inches maybe be retained
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than three (3) spotted seatrout per person per day
taken by hook-and-line or for recreational purposes.
II. COMMERCIAL SIZE AND HARVEST RESTRICTIONS
A. It shall be unlawful to possess spotted seatrout taken for commercial purposes less
than 14 inches total length.
B. It shall be unlawful for a commercial fishing operation to possess more than 75 spotted
seatrout per day except the stop net beach seine fishery in the Atlantic Ocean off
Bogue Banks, Carteret County.
C. It shall be unlawful for a commercial fishing operation to possess more than three (3)
spotted seatrout per person per day taken by hook and line.
D. From January 1 through September 30, it shall be unlawful for a commercial fishing
operation to take or land spotted seatrout for commercial purposes from 11:59 P.M.
Friday to 12:01 A.M. Monday each week.
E. From October 1 through December 31, it shall be unlawful to take or land spotted
seatrout for commercial purposes from 11:59 P.M. Friday through 12:01 A.M. Tuesday
each week.

A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G. S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine
Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103, 03M .0512 and 03M .0522.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-130 to “Take” as used in this
proclamation is defined as all operations during, immediately preparatory, and
immediately subsequent to an attempt, whether successful or not, to capture, kill,
pursue, hunt, or otherwise harm or reduce to possession any fisheries resources or
wildlife resources.
D. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC
03I .0101(5)(f)(ii) to “Land” as used in this proclamation is defined for trip tickets, when
fish reach a licensed seafood dealer, or when the fisherman is the dealer, when the
fish reach the shore or a structure connected to the shore.
E. For additional restrictions on spotted seatrout harvest with stop nets refer to the most
recent FF-type proclamation that establishes the reporting requirements and spotted
seatrout harvest restrictions for stop nets in the Atlantic Ocean off Bogue Banks,
Carteret County, in accordance with Amendment 1 to the N.C. Spotted Seatrout
Fishery Management Plan.
F. For additional stop net restrictions refer to the most recent M-type proclamation that
establishes the areas, seasons, gear, and additional restrictions including the net
operation call in requirements in the stop net beach seine fishery in the Atlantic Ocean
off Bogue Banks, Carteret County, in accordance with Amendment 2 to the N.C.
Striped Mullet Fishery Management Plan.
G. The intent of this proclamation is to manage the spotted seatrout fishery in accordance
with Amendment 1 to the N.C. Spotted Seatrout Fishery Management Plan.
H. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c) all persons who may be affected
by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep themselves
informed of current proclamations.
I. Contact the N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC
28557, phone 252-515-5500 or 800-682-2632 for more information or visit the division
website at www.ncmarinefisheries.net.
J. This proclamation opens the commercial and recreational spotted seatrout
(speckled trout) fishery in Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters including the
Atlantic Ocean on July 1, 2026, in accordance with Amendment 1 to the N.C.
Spotted Seatrout Fishery Management Plan.
