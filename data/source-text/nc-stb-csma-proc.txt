PROCLAMATION
RE: ESTUARINE STRIPED BASS – RECREATIONAL – SEASON OPEN – PORTIONS OF TAR-
PAMLICO AND NEUSE RIVERS INTERNAL COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING WATERS
This proclamation supersedes FF-6-2019, dated March 26, 2019. This proclamation opens
portions of the Tar-Pamlico and Neuse rivers in Internal Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters of
the Central Southern Management Area (CSMA) to the harvest of striped bass (including
hybrids) for recreational purposes, sets the 2026 recreational season and harvest limits, and
maintains the striped bass harvest closure in the remaining areas of the CSMA in accordance
with the 2026 Revision to Amendment 2 to the North Carolina Estuarine Striped Bass Fishery
Management Plan.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01 A.M.,
Wednesday, April 1, 2026, the following restrictions shall apply to the taking of striped bass for
recreational purposes in Internal Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters of the Central Southern
Management Area (CSMA):
I. AREA DESCRIPTION (See Map)
A. Tar-Pamlico River: All Internal Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters west of a line beginning
at a point on the north shore 35° 25.1669’ N - 76° 45.5251’ W (Gum Point); running
southwesterly to a point on the south shore 35° 23.4453’ N - 76° 46.4346’ W (Fork
Point).
B. Neuse River: All Internal Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters west of a line beginning at a
point on the north shore 35° 02.1433’ N - 76° 55.9965’ W (Cooper Point); running
southwesterly to a point on the south shore at 35° 00.1550’ N - 76° 58.5738’ W (Fisher
Landing Point).
II. SEASON
It shall be unlawful to possess striped bass in the Central Southern Management Area (CSMA),
as defined in N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03R .0201 (c), except for
recreational purposes from 12:01 A.M. on Wednesday April 1, 2026, through 11:59 P.M. on
Thursday, April 30, 2026, in the areas described in Section I.
III. SIZE AND CREEL LIMITS
In the areas and during the times described in Sections I and II:
A. It shall be unlawful to possess striped bass less than 18 inches total length.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess striped bass between 22- and 27-inches total length.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess more than one (1) striped bass per person per day or
per trip, whichever is more restrictive.

IV. MEANS AND METHODS
A. Striped bass may be taken by hook and line or with recreational commercial gear for
recreational purposes seven days a week during the open season.
B. It shall be unlawful to sell, offer for sale, or purchase striped bass taken for recreational
purposes by hook and line or with recreational commercial gear.
V. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-170.4; 113-
170.5; 113-182; 113.182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103; 03M .0202; 03M .0512; and 03Q. 0107.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission
Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c) All persons who may be affected
by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep themselves
informed of current proclamations.
D. This proclamation, which is consistent with the North Carolina Wildlife Resources
Commission proclamation issued on March 20, 2026, only applies to Internal Coastal
and Joint Fishing Waters of the Central Southern Management Area (CSMA).
E. For Recreational Commercial Gear License refer to M-type proclamations for gill net
regulations that may be effective in the areas covered by this proclamation and M-type
proclamations for Estuarine Gill Net Permit and Observer Trip Scheduling System (refer
to M-4-2026 for reference).
F. Holders of Recreational Commercial Gear Licenses (RCGL) must follow the bag and
harvest restrictions of the recreational fishery for striped bass. Commercial fishing
equipment authorized by the Recreational Commercial Gear License is not considered
commercial fishing equipment in the striped bass fishery and it is illegal to sell or
purchase striped bass taken by this method in accordance with N.C.G.S. 113-173.
G. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03M .0201 defines striped bass as
striped bass and its hybrids
H. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03Q .0107 states that it is unlawful
to possess striped bass or striped bass hybrids less than 18 inches total length and
between the lengths of 22- and 27-inches total length in Internal Joint Fishing Waters
of the CSMA.
I. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03M .0202 and 15A NCAC
03Q .0107 state that it is unlawful to possess striped bass from the Internal Coastal and
Joint Fishing Waters of the Cape Fear River and its tributaries, including Snow’s Cut.
J. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 3I .0124 requires recreational
fisherman who harvest flounder, red drum, spotted seatrout, striped bass, and
weakfish from Coastal, Joint, and Inland Fishing Waters or Inland Fishing Waters
adjacent to Internal Coastal or Joint Fishing Waters to report that harvest to the
N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries.
K. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03M .0202 states that all striped
bass that do not meet the minimum size limit specified by proclamation shall
immediately be returned to the waters from which taken regardless of condition.
L. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221(f) and 113-221.1(c) all persons who
may be affected by rules and proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under
a duty to keep themselves informed of current rules and proclamations.
M. Contact N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC 28557,
252-515-5500 or 800-682-2632 for more information or visit the division website at
https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries.
