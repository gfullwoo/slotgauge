SH-4-2022
PROCLAMATION
RE: SHRIMP – RECREATIONAL CAST NET HARVEST – COASTAL FISHING WATERS
This proclamation removes the recreational shrimp harvest limit in areas closed to shrimp
harvest to establish a consistent recreational harvest limit for cast nets in all Coastal
Fishing Waters, in accordance with Amendment 2 to the North Carolina Shrimp Fishery
Management Plan.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 5:01
P.M., Sunday, May 15, 2022 the following restrictions will apply to the recreational harvest of
shrimp when using a cast net.
I. SUSPENSION OF A PORTION OF N.C. MARINE FISHERIES COMMISSION RULE 15A
NCAC 03L .0105.
The following portion of N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03L .0105
is suspended:
Section (2) which reads:
(2) Take or possess more than four quarts, heads on or two and one-half quarts,
heads off, of shrimp per person per day with a cast net from areas closed to the
taking of shrimp.
II. RECREATIONAL HARVEST LIMIT
It is unlawful to possess more than 48 quarts, heads on, or 30 quarts, heads off, of shrimp
per person per day or if a vessel is used, per vessel per day with a cast net for recreational
purposes in all Coastal Fishing Waters.
III. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-170.4;
113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03L .0101.
B. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103
it is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority.
C. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c) All persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep
themselves informed of current proclamations.
D. Contact N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC 28557;
252-726-7021 or 800-682-2632 for more information or visit the division website at
www.ncmarinefisheries.net.
E. The intent of this proclamation is to manage the shrimp fishery in accordance with
Amendment 2 to the North Carolina Shrimp Fishery Management Plan.
F. This proclamation removes the recreational shrimp harvest limit in areas
