 Update to Red Drum Regulations (effective July 1, 2018)
 The South Carolina General Assembly has passed a new law intended to reverse Red Drum overfishing. The lowered bag limit allows two fish per person per 
 day and no more than six fish per boat per day. The previous catch limit was three fish per person per day, with no boat limit. The slot limit (15-23 inches) 
 remains unchanged.
Slot Limit:
15-23 inches (total length)
Bag Limit: 
2 per person per day, and no more than 6 per boat per day
