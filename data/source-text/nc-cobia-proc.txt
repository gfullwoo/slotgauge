FF-4-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE: COBIA – COMMERCIAL AND RECREATIONAL – COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING
WATERS
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-2-2025 dated December 16, 2024. This
proclamation opens the commercial cobia fishery beginning at 12:01 A.M. on January 1,
2026. The season for the recreational harvest of cobia will re-open at 12:01 A.M. on May 1,
2026.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01
A.M., January 1, 2026, the following restrictions will apply to the cobia fishery in Coastal and
Joint Fishing Waters:
I. COMMERCIAL SIZE AND HARVEST LIMIT
A. It shall be unlawful to possess cobia less than 36 inches fork length in a commercial
operation.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than two (2) cobia per person per day or six (6)
per vessel per day, whichever is more restrictive in a commercial operation.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess a commercial and recreational limit of cobia on the
same vessel.
D. The commercial fishery will close by proclamation once the quota is met or projected
to be met, or December 31, 2026, whichever occurs first.
II. RECREATIONAL SEASON, HARVEST, VESSEL, AND SIZE LIMIT
A. RECREATIONAL SIZE LIMIT, POSSESSION LIMIT, AND SEASON
1. The season for the recreational harvest of cobia will be May 1, 2026 –
December 31, 2026. It shall be unlawful to possess cobia outside of this
season.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess for a recreational purpose cobia less than 36
inches fork length.
3. It shall be unlawful to possess for a recreational purpose more than one (1)
cobia per person per day (See section B and C below for season and vessel
limits).
B. PRIVATE VESSEL LIMITS for Recreational Purpose (All vessels not engaged in
a For-Hire Vessel operation)
1. From May 1 – June 30, it shall be unlawful to possess more than two (2)
cobia per vessel per day if two or more persons are onboard or one (1) cobia
per person per day, if there is only one person on board.
2. From July 1 – December 31, it shall be unlawful to possess more than one
(1) cobia per vessel per day.

C. FOR-HIRE VESSEL (While engaged in a For-Hire Vessel operation as defined by
G.S 113-174)
1. From May 1 – December 31, it shall be unlawful to possess more than four
(4) cobia per vessel per day or (1) cobia per person per day, if fewer than
four (4) people are on board.
III. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113.134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52; and N.C. Marine
Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H.0103.
C. Fishermen are encouraged to return all cobia not meeting harvest requirements to the
water in a manner that ensures that highest likelihood of survival.
D. The intent of this proclamation is to manage the commercial and recreational fishery
for cobia in Coastal Fishing Waters consistent with the Atlantic States Marine
Fisheries Commission’s Interstate Cobia Fishery Management Plan.
E. Contact the N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC
28557; phone 252-515-5500 or 800-682-2632 for more information or visit the division
website at https://deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries.
F. In accordance with N.C.G.S. 113-221.1(c) all persons who may be affected by
proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep themselves
informed of current proclamations.
G. This proclamation opens the commercial cobia fishery beginning at 12:01 A.M.
on January 1, 2026. The season for the recreational harvest of cobia will re-open
at 12:01 A.M. on May 1, 2026.
