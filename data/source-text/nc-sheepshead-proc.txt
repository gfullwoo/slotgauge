FF-16-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE: SHEEPSHEAD – RECREATIONAL AND COMMERCIAL – COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING
WATERS
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-14-2026 dated February 16, 2026. This
proclamation clarifies the commercial trip limits and maintains the minimum size limit, the
length measurement in total length, commercial trip limit for the pound net fishery and other
commercial gears, and the recreational creel limit for the sheepshead fishery in Coastal and
Joint Fishing Waters.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01 A.M.,
March 16, 2026, the following restrictions will apply to the recreational and commercial sheepshead
fisheries in Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters.
I. MINIMUM SIZE LIMIT
It shall be unlawful to possess sheepshead less than 14 inches total length.
II. RECREATIONAL HARVEST LIMIT
It shall be unlawful to possess for recreational purposes no more than 5 sheepshead per person
per day or per person per trip, whichever is more restrictive.
III. COMMERCIAL HARVEST LIMIT
It shall be unlawful for a commercial fishing operation consisting of one or more SCFL/RSCFL
holders to take, possess, or land more than 300 pounds of sheepshead per day or per trip,
whichever is more restrictive, except:
1. It shall be unlawful for commercial gig and spear operations to take, possess, or land
more than 10 sheepshead per person per day or per trip, whichever is more restrictive.
2. It shall be unlawful for commercial pound net operations consisting of one or more
SCFL/RSCFL holders to take, possess, or land more than 1,500 pounds of
sheepshead per day or per trip, whichever is more restrictive.
IV. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03M .0521.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission
Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c), all persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep
themselves informed of current proclamations.

D. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC
03I .0101(1)(d)(iv) Total Length means a length determined by measuring along a
straight line the distance from the tip of the snout with the mouth closed to the tip of
the compressed caudal (tail) fin.
E. Fishermen are encouraged to return all sheepshead that do not meet harvest
requirements immediately to the waters where taken in a manner that ensures the
highest likelihood of survival.
F. The intent of this proclamation is to manage sheepshead in Coastal and Joint Fishing
Waters.
G. Contact the N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC
28557; 252-515-5500 or 800-682-2632 or for more information visit the division
website at https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries.
H. This proclamation clarifies the commercial trip limits and maintains the
minimum size limit, the length measurement in total length, commercial trip limit
for the pound net fishery and other commercial gears, and the recreational creel
limit for the sheepshead fishery in Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters.
