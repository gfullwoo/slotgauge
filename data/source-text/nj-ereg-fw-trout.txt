 Trout Fishing Regulations
 Last Updated: September 23, 2026
Topic
Previous (2025)
New (2026)
Opening Day of Trout Season 
Set annually with no fixed rule; opening day was April 5, 2025. 
Now defined as the second Saturday in April every year; opening day is April 11, 2026. 
Preseason Closure – Lakes and Ponds 
Lakes and ponds closed to all fishing for the three weeks before opening day, same as rivers and streams. 
Preseason closure removed for lakes and ponds; they now stay open to fishing year-round. 
Trout Daily Limit – Before Opening Day 
Daily limit was 4 trout from Jan. 1 through Mar. 16, then closed or catch-and-release during the 3-week preseason. 
Daily limit reduced to 2 trout; lakes and ponds now stay open at this limit straight through to opening day. 
Trout Daily Limit – June 1 to Dec. 31 
Daily limit was 4 trout. 
Daily limit reduced to 2 trout. 
Pequannock River – Brown Trout Protection 
No special regulation on this section; standard 9-inch minimum size applied. 
New for 2026: “Trout Stocked and Wild Brown Trout Managed” section from Rt. 23 (Smith Mills) to the railroad trestle below Appelt Park. Brown Trout minimum size increased from 9 to 12 inches, and only 2 of the 6-fish daily trout limit may be Brown Trout. 
Lake Trout – Merrill Creek Reservoir 
Daily limit was 2 fish at a 15-inch minimum, with a catch-and-release closure Sept. 16 to Nov. 30. 
Daily limit increased to 4 fish total (2 fish 15 to under 24 inches plus 2 fish 24 inches or greater); catch-and-release closure removed, harvest now open year-round. 
Lake Trout – Round Valley Reservoir 
Daily limit was 7 fish total (6 fish 15 to under 24 inches plus 1 fish 24 inches or greater), with a catch-and-release closure Sept. 16 to Nov. 30. 
Daily limit increased to 9 fish total (8 fish 15 to under 24 inches plus 1 fish 24 inches or greater); catch-and-release closure removed, harvest now open year-round. 
 Regulations in red are new this year.

 Map of the New Jersey Brook Trout Conservation Zone highlighting designated counties where all brook trout caught must be immediately released. The conservation area protects native brook trout populations and supports sustainable freshwater fishing management across northern New Jersey.
 Trout Stocked Waters Closed to Fishing on Spring In-season Stocking Dates
 After opening day, these waters are closed to fishing from 5 a.m. to 5 p.m. on their designated stocking dates. Closures apply to all tributaries for 100 feet from the main channel. There are no closures for ANY waters in the fall. These waters or portions of these waters are also stocked in the fall.
