FF-40-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE: SNAPPER GROUPER COMPLEX – RECREATIONAL – RED SNAPPER OPEN LIMITED
SEASON
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-29-2026 dated June 24, 2026. This
proclamation opens a limited recreational red snapper season in 2026 only for October 9,
10, and 11 (Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
Please see the following NOAA Fishery Bulletin for more information:
https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/bulletin/noaa-fisheries-announces-2026-federal-
recreational-red-snapper-season-and-issuance
Michael S. Loeffler, Interim Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective
12:01 A.M., October 9, 2026, the following restrictions will apply to the recreational fisheries for
snapper grouper:
I. Permit Requirements
A. It shall be unlawful to sell or offer for sale any species of the snapper grouper complex
taken or possessed under the recreational bag limits specified in 50 CFR Part
§622.187(b) unless the vessel owner has been issued a valid Federal Commercial
South Atlantic Snapper Grouper Permit.
B. For a vessel with both a valid Federal South Atlantic Charter/Headboat Permit for
snapper grouper and a valid Federal Commercial South Atlantic Snapper Grouper
Permit it shall be unlawful to exceed the recreational bag limits for snapper grouper
species when fishing with more than four persons (including captain and mate) on
board, or when carrying paying customers.
C. For a vessel with both a valid Federal South Atlantic Charter/Headboat Permit for
snapper grouper and a valid Federal Commercial South Atlantic Snapper Grouper
Permit it shall be unlawful to sell or offer for sale any species of the snapper grouper
complex harvested when fishing with more than four persons (including captain and
mate) on board or when carrying paying customers.
II. Black Sea Bass, south of Cape Hatteras (35° 15.0321’ N. Latitude)
A. It shall be unlawful to possess black sea bass less than 13 inches total length (tip of
the snout to the tip of the tail, EXCLUDING the filament at the end of the tail).
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than seven (7) black sea bass per person per day.
III. Groupers
A. It shall be unlawful to possess speckled hind, warsaw grouper, Nassau grouper, and
goliath grouper.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess black grouper or gag grouper less than 24 inches total
length.

C. It shall be unlawful to possess red grouper, yellowfin (fireback) grouper, yellowmouth
grouper or scamp less than 20 inches total length.
D. It shall be unlawful to possess the following grouper species taken from the waters
under the jurisdiction of North Carolina or the South Atlantic Fishery Management
Council except from May 1 through December 31 each year: gag grouper, black
grouper, rock hind, red hind, coney, graysby, yellowfin grouper, scamp and
yellowmouth grouper.
E. It shall be unlawful to possess snowy grouper taken from waters under the jurisdiction
of North Carolina or the South Atlantic Fishery Management Council. The snowy
grouper recreational season will re-open May 1, 2027.
F. It shall be unlawful to possess red grouper except from June 1 through December 31
each year, subject to the bag limits in Section XVII. B.
G. It shall be unlawful to possess gag grouper except from 12:01 A.M. on May 1, 2026
through 11:59 P.M. on August 1,2026, subject to the bag and vessel limits in Section
XVII. B
H. It shall be unlawful to possess from a private vessel more than two (2) gag and two
black grouper per vessel per day not to exceed the daily limit of one (1) gag or one (1)
black grouper (but not both) per person per day, whichever is more restrictive, subject
to the bag limits in Section XVII. B.
I. It shall be unlawful to possess from a charter/headboat (for-hire) vessel more than
two (2) gag grouper and two black grouper per vessel per day not to exceed the daily
limit of one (1) gag or one (1) black grouper (but not both) per person per day,
whichever is more restrictive, subject to the bag limits in Section XVII. B.
J. It shall be unlawful for the captain and crew on a for-hire vessel with a Federal or
For-Hire Snapper-Grouper Permit to retain gag or black grouper.
IV. Greater Amberjack
A. It shall be unlawful to possess greater amberjack less than 28 inches fork length.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than one (1) greater amberjack per person per
day.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess greater amberjack taken from waters under the
jurisdiction of North Carolina or the South Atlantic Fishery Management Council from
April 1 through April 30 each year.
V. Other Jacks
The Other Jacks Complex includes almaco jack, banded rudderfish, and lesser amberjack.
These species are included in the twenty (20) fish aggregate per person per day recreational
bag and possession limit in Section XVII. C.
VI. Red Snapper
It shall be unlawful to possess red snapper taken from waters under the jurisdiction of North
Carolina or the South Atlantic Fishery Management Council. Effective only from 12:01 A.M
October 9, 2026, through 11:59 P.M., October 11, 2026 it shall be unlawful to possess
more than one red snapper per person per day. There is no minimum size limit.
VII. Vermilion Snapper
A. It shall be unlawful to possess vermilion snapper (beeliner) less than 12 inches total
length.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than five (5) vermilion snapper per person per
day.

VIII. Hogfish (hog snapper)
A. It shall be unlawful to possess hogfish less than 17 inches fork length.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than two (2) hogfish per person per day.
IX. Other Snappers
A. It shall be unlawful to possess yellowtail snapper, cubera snapper, and gray snapper
less than 12 inches total length.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess mutton snapper less than 18 inches total length.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess lane snapper less than 8 inches total length.
X. Deepwater Complex
A. The Deepwater Complex includes yellowedge grouper, silk snapper, misty grouper,
queen snapper, sand tilefish, and blackfin snapper.
B. Silk snapper, queen snapper and blackfin snapper are included in the ten (10) fish
aggregate per person per day recreational bag and possession limit in Section XVII.
A.
C. Yellowedge grouper, misty grouper and sand tilefish are included in the three (3) fish
aggregate per person per day recreational bag and possession limit in Section XVII.
B.
XI. Red Porgy
A. It shall be unlawful to possess red porgy less than 14 inches total length.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than one (1) red porgy per person per day, or one
(1) red porgy per person per trip, whichever is more restrictive.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess red porgy except from May 1 through June 30 each
year.
XII. Other Porgies
The Porgies Complex includes jolthead porgy, knobbed porgy, whitebone porgy, saucereye
porgy, and scup. These species are included in the twenty (20) fish in the aggregate per
person per day recreational bag and possession limit in Section XVII. C.
XIII. Golden Tilefish
It shall be unlawful to possess golden tilefish taken from waters under the jurisdiction of
North Carolina or the South Atlantic Fishery Management Council. The golden tilefish
recreational season will re-open January 1, 2027.
XIV. Blueline Tilefish
A. It shall be unlawful to possess blueline tilefish except from May 1 through August 31
each year, subject to the bag limits in Section XVII. B.
B. It shall be unlawful for the captain and crew on a for-hire vessel with a Federal or
For-Hire Snapper-Grouper Permit to retain blueline tilefish.
XV. Gray Triggerfish
It shall be unlawful to possess gray triggerfish less than 12 inches fork length and is subject
to the bag limits in Section XVII. C.
XVI. Wreckfish
A. It shall be unlawful to possess more than one (1) wreckfish per vessel per day.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess wreckfish except from July 1 through August 31 of each
year.

XVII. Combined Bag Limits
A. It shall be unlawful to possess more than ten (10) of the following species of snappers
in the aggregate per person per day when the recreational season for each species is
open: silk snapper, blackfin snapper, yellowtail snapper, cubera snapper, queen
snapper, gray snapper, mutton snapper and lane snapper. No more than five (5) may
be mutton snapper per person per day within the ten (10) fish snapper aggregate.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess more than three (3) of the following species of groupers
or tilefish in the aggregate per person per day when the recreational season for each
species is open: gag grouper, black grouper, snowy grouper, misty grouper, red
grouper, rock hind, red hind, coney, graysby, yellowedge grouper, yellowfin grouper,
scamp, yellowmouth grouper; blueline tilefish, golden tilefish, and sand tilefish with the
following restrictions:
1. No more than one (1) may be a gag or black grouper (but not both) per person
per day.
2. No more than two (2) gag grouper and two (2) black grouper per vessel per
day.
3. No more than one (1) golden tilefish per person per day.
4. No more than two (2) blueline tilefish per person per day.
5. No more than one (1) snowy grouper per vessel per day.
6. No more than one (1) may be a scamp or yellowmouth grouper (but not both)
per person per day.
C. It shall be unlawful to possess more than ten (10) of the following species of fish within
the twenty (20) fish per person per day aggregate bag limit when the recreational
season for each species is open: whitebone porgy, jolthead porgy, knobbed porgy,
saucereye porgy, scup (south of Cape Hatteras, 35° 15.0321’ N. Latitude), gray
triggerfish, bar jack, almaco jack, banded rudderfish, lesser amberjack, white grunt,
margate, sailor’s choice and spadefish.
XVIII. Harvest Restrictions
A. It shall be unlawful to fail to use dehooking tools in the snapper grouper fisheries when
the fish is to be released as specified in 50 CFR Part §622.188.
B. It shall be unlawful to possess any species of the snapper grouper complex without
heads and fins intact as specified in 50 CFR Part §622.186.
XIX. Gear Modifications
A. It shall be unlawful to fail to use non-offset, non-stainless steel circle hooks (50 CFR
Part §622.2) while fishing for or possessing snapper-grouper species while using
natural baits as specified in 50 CFR Part §622.188. A non-offset hook has the point
and barb in-line with the hook shank (See diagrams).
B. It shall be unlawful to fail to possess a descending device on board which is readily
available for use while fishing for or possessing snapper-grouper species as specified

XX. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C. Marine
Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103, 03M .0506 and 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. The intent of this proclamation is to maintain North Carolina’s compliance with the
requirements of amendments to the Fishery Management Plan for the Snapper
Grouper Fishery of the South Atlantic Region.
D. The South Atlantic Fishery Management Council has identified best practices for
fisherman to reduce the release mortality of red snapper and other snapper/grouper
species. A list of these practices can be found in the NOAA Fishery Bulletin:
https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/bulletin/noaa-fisheries-announces-limited-opening-
recreational-and-commercial-red-snapper. Anglers are encouraged to release fish in
a manner that encourages the highest likelihood of survival.
E. Examples and further descriptions of descending devices can be found in the NOAA
Fishery Bulletin: https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/bulletin/noaa-fisheries-announces-
gear-modifications-snapper-grouper-fishery.
F. Anglers are asked to collect information about their recreational trips and catches
through the SAFMC Release project using the mobile app SciFish (available for free
download at the Google Play and App Store). To participate, visit the SAFMC Release
website https://safmc.net/citizen-science/safmc-release/ to set up an account.
G. Provisions for multiple day trip possessions for vessels without a valid Federal South
Atlantic Snapper Grouper Permit are found in 50 CFR Part §622.187(c). This federal
rule states in part that a person aboard a charter vessel or headboat on a trip that
spans more than 24 hours may possess no more than two daily bag limits of species

other than red porgy. A person aboard a headboat on a trip that spans more than 48
hours and who can document that fishing was conducted on at least three days may
possess no more than three bag limits of species other than red porgy. Privately-
operated vessels are allowed only one bag limit.
H. Effective January 27, 2014, federal rules are amended to allow captains and crew of
for-hire vessels with valid Federal South Atlantic Charter/Headboat Snapper Grouper
Permits to retain bag limit quantities of all snapper grouper species except for blueline
tilefish, gag and black grouper during the open recreational season.
I. Fish harvested under the recreational bag limits may not be sold as part of a state-
licensed recreational fishing tournament (50 CFR Part §622.192).
J. Vessels fishing in or harvesting fish from the EEZ are required to carry the appropriate
federal permit when operating as a for-hire vessel. See 50 CFR Part §622.170(b).
K. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c), all persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep
themselves informed of current proclamations.
L. Contact the N.C. Division of Marine Fisheries, P.O. Box 769, Morehead City, NC
28557; phone 252-515-5500 or 800-682-2632 for more information, or visit the division
website at https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries. For a quick guide
to snapper grouper regulations, please see https://safmc.net/regulations/.
M. For the recreational sector, if the Regional Administrator (RA) for NOAA Fisheries
Southeast Regional Office determines that weather conditions classified by the
National Weather Service at least as severe as a small craft advisory exist, or are
projected to exist, in the South Atlantic EEZ, the RA may modify the opening and
closing dates of the red snapper recreational fishing season by filing a notification to
that effect with the Office of the Federal Register and announcing via NOAA Weather
Radio and a Fishery Bulletin any change in the dates of the red snapper recreational
fishing season (50 CFR 622.183(b)(5)(iii)).
N. This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-29-2026 dated June 24, 2026.
This proclamation opens a limited recreational red snapper season in 2026 only
for October 9, 10, and 11 (Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
