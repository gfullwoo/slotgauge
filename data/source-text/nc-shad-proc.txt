FF-8-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE: SHAD – COMMERCIAL AND RECREATIONAL – COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING
WATERS INCLUDING THE ATLANTIC OCEAN – TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF RULES
This proclamation supersedes proclamation FF-8-2025, dated December 18, 2024. This
proclamation sets the 2026 commercial and recreational seasons and harvest restrictions
for the taking of American shad and hickory shad in Coastal and Joint Fishing Waters
including the Atlantic Ocean.
Kathy B. Rawls, Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that effective at 12:01
A.M., Thursday, January 1, 2026, the following restrictions will apply in Coastal and Joint Fishing
Waters including the Atlantic Ocean to the taking of American shad and hickory shad for
commercial and recreational purposes:
I. TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF RULES
A. The following portion of N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC
03Q .0107 is suspended:
Paragraph (4) which reads:
(4) Shad: It is unlawful to possess more than 10 American shad or hickory shad,
in the aggregate, per person per day taken by hook-and-line.
II. HARVEST RESTRICTIONS-COMMERCIAL (SEE SECTION IV. and V. FOR
ADDITIONAL RESTRICTIONS)
A. CAPE FEAR RIVER AND TRIBUTARIES
1. It shall be unlawful to possess American shad for commercial purposes prior
to 12:01 A.M. Friday, February 20, 2026, and after 11:59 P.M. Saturday, April
11, 2026.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess hickory shad for commercial purposes prior to
12:01 A.M., Thursday, January 1, 2026, and after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.
B. ATLANTIC OCEAN
1. It shall be unlawful to possess hickory shad for commercial purposes prior to
12:01 A.M., Thursday, January 1, 2026, and after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.
C. ALL OTHER INTERNAL COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING WATERS
1. It shall be unlawful to possess American shad for commercial purposes prior
to 12:01 A.M. Sunday, February 15, 2026, and after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess hickory shad for commercial purposes prior to
12:01 A.M., Thursday, January 1, 2026, and after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.

III. HARVEST RESTRICTIONS-RECREATIONAL
A. PAMLICO AND PUNGO RIVERS AND TRIBUTARIES
1. It shall be unlawful to possess more than ten (10) American shad or hickory
shad, in the aggregate, per person per day for recreational purposes.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess American shad or hickory shad for recreational
purposes by any method except hook-and-line after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.
B. CAPE FEAR RIVER AND TRIBUTARIES
1. It shall be unlawful to possess more than ten (10) American shad or hickory
shad in the aggregate, per person per day for recreational purposes and only
five (5) of the ten (10) may be an American shad.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess American shad or hickory shad for recreational
purposes by any method except hook-and-line after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.
C. ATLANTIC OCEAN
1. It shall be unlawful to possess more than ten (10) hickory shad per person per
day for recreational purposes.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess hickory shad for recreational purposes by any
method except hook-and-line after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April 14, 2026.
D. ALL OTHER INTERNAL COASTAL AND JOINT FISHING WATERS
1. It shall be unlawful to possess more than ten (10) American shad or hickory
shad in the aggregate, per person per day for recreational purposes and only
one (1) of the ten (10) may be an American shad.
2. It shall be unlawful to possess American shad or hickory shad for recreational
purposes by any method except hook-and-line after 11:59 P.M., Tuesday, April
14, 2026.
IV. TRANSPORTATION EXEMPTION
Commercial fishing operations or recreational harvest shall be exempt from the
transportation requirement set out in 15A NCAC 03I .0120 when transporting shad in
waterbodies that are closed to harvest. Commercial fishing operations and recreational
harvest are not exempt when in possession of shad and harvesting shad in a closed area.
This exemption is from 12:01 A.M., Thursday, January 1, 2026, to 11:59 P.M., Tuesday,
April 14, 2026.
V. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C.G.S. 113-134; 113-134.1; 113-
170.4; 113-170.5; 113-181; 113-182; 113-182.1 113-221.1; 143B-289.52 and N.C.
Marine Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03M .0512.
B. It is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority pursuant to N.C. Marine Fisheries
Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03H .0103.
C. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03M .0519, it
shall be unlawful to possess American shad taken from the Atlantic Ocean.
D. In accordance with N.C. General Statute 113-221.1(c), all persons who may be
affected by proclamations issued by the Fisheries Director are under a duty to keep
themselves informed of current proclamations.
E. The intent of this proclamation is to implement the provisions of the N.C. American
Shad Sustainable Fishery Plan as part of Amendment 3 to the Atlantic States Marine
