# Google Play listing — SlotGauge

## App name (30)
SlotGauge: Is it a keeper?

## Short description (80)
Say, snap or type your catch. Keep or release by today's state rules, plus tides.

## Full description (4000)
Caught a fish and not sure if you can keep it? Tell SlotGauge what you caught — say it, snap a photo, or type it — and get a clear KEEP or RELEASE answer with the exact size limit, season and daily limit from your state's official regulations.

WHAT IT DOES
• Say it: "twenty-two inch striper in the bay" — the mic turns itself off when you stop talking.
• Snap it: take a photo and SlotGauge identifies the species.
• Type it: species, length and where you are.
• Answers use today's date, your zone (ocean, bay, river, inland) and the current season, slot and bag limits.
• Shows the exact wording from the state agency and a link to the source page.

STATES COVERED
Delaware, New Jersey, Maryland, Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida, New York and Connecticut — saltwater and statewide inland rules. SlotGauge picks your state from your location, or choose any state to plan a trip. Rules are rechecked against the official agency pages automatically.

TIDES
A tide badge at the top shows whether the tide is rising or falling and when the next high or low is. Tap it for a 48-hour chart, highs and lows, and any NOAA station in the country — your nearest one by default, or search a town, pier or inlet to plan travel.

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• Tide (1×1): rising/falling arrow and the next high or low, refreshed automatically.
• Tide + Say it (2×1): the same plus a microphone button that opens SlotGauge already listening.
Long-press a widget to pin it to any tide station.

ALSO
• Species guides for every regulated fish in each state, with photos and background.
• Works offline with the last saved regulations.
• Optional Google sign-in to keep a gallery of your catches.
• No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

SlotGauge simplifies regulations for quick checks. The state agency's current fishing guide governs, and gear, license and area rules also apply. Not legal advice.
