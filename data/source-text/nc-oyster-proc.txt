SF-1-2026
PROCLAMATION
RE : 2026-2027 OYSTER HAND HARVEST RESTRICTIONS
This proclamation opens the season for harvest of oysters by hand methods from public
bottom in Internal Coastal Fishing Waters and reflects harvest limits by license type in
accordance with the N.C. Oyster Fishery Management Plan Amendment 5. The harvest of
oysters by hand methods from public bottom in Internal Coastal Fishing Waters will close
on March 31, 2027, in accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC
03K .0201, unless closed sooner by proclamation.
Michael S. Loeffler, Interim Director, Division of Marine Fisheries, hereby announces that
effective at sunrise, October 15, 2026, the following restrictions shall apply to the harvest of
oysters by hand methods from public bottom in Internal Coastal Fishing Waters:
I. HARVEST RESTRICTIONS
A. MEANS AND METHODS
1. Oysters may be taken by hand and hand operated implements only
(excluding dredges pulled by hand except as described below in (5); see
General Information Section IV. for further gear restrictions).
2. It shall be unlawful to transfer oysters from the catching vessel to any other
vessel during oyster harvesting operations or to transport oysters by any
vessel other than the vessel in which they were harvested.
3. It shall be unlawful to possess commercial and recreational limits of oysters
aboard the same vessel.
4. It shall be unlawful to sell oysters on Saturday and Sunday taken from public
bottom.
5. It shall be unlawful to use any dredge to take oysters except on shellfish
leases and franchises by permit or in an area that is open for mechanical
harvest of oysters.
B. HARVEST DAYS
Oyster harvest shall be allowed Monday through Friday of each week except
as provided in Section I.E. below.

number of persons holding a Standard Commercial Fishing License with an
endorsement to sell shellfish or vessels that may be involved, in Internal

D. SHELLFISH LICENSE HARVEST LIMITS
It shall be unlawful for persons holding a Shellfish License to take or possess
more than two (2) bushels of oysters per day and shall not exceed a total of four
(4) bushels per vessel in any combined commercial fishing operation from public
bottom, regardless of the number of persons holding a Shellfish License
onboard the vessel in Internal Coastal Fishing Waters statewide.
E. RECREATONAL HARVEST LIMITS
It shall be unlawful for unlicensed persons and harvesters taking oysters for
personal consumption to take or possess more than one (1) bushel of oysters
per person per day not to exceed two (2) bushels per vessel per day for
recreational purposes seven days a week in Internal Coastal Fishing Waters
from public bottom (see Means and Methods Section I. A.3. above).
F. MINIMUM SIZE LIMIT
It shall be unlawful to possess oysters less than three (3) inches shell length for
oysters taken on public or private bottom (see General Information Section
IV.D.).

IV. GENERAL INFORMATION
A. This proclamation is issued under the authority of N.C. General Statutes 113-134;
113-170.4; 113-170.5; 113-182; 113-182.1; 113-221.1; 143B-289.52; and N.C.
Marine Fisheries Commission Rules 15A NCAC 03H .0103 and 03K .0201.
B. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 3H .0103,
it is unlawful to violate the provisions of any proclamation issued by the Fisheries
Director under their delegated authority.
C. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03K .0102 prohibits the taking
of oysters with rakes more than twelve (12) inches wide or weighing more than six
(6) pounds.
D. N.C Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03K .0207 exempts permitted
aquaculture operations from bag and size limit restrictions.
E. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 03K .0106, it shall be
unlawful to take oysters between the hours of sunset and sunrise on any day.
F. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03K .0202,
It shall be unlawful to possess oysters that have accumulated dead shell,
accumulated oyster cultch material, a shell length less than that specified by
proclamation issued under the authority of N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule
15A NCAC 3K .0201, or any combination thereof that exceeds a five-percent
tolerance limit by volume. Oysters shall be culled where harvested and all oysters
of less than legal size, accumulated dead shell, and cultch material shall be
immediately returned to the bottom from which it was taken.
G. In accordance with N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03K .0205,
it shall be unlawful for shellfish lease or franchise holders or their designees to take
or possess oysters from public bottom while possessing aboard a vessel oysters
taken from shellfish leases or franchises.
H. N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03K .0209 prohibits the taking
of oysters from oyster sanctuaries designated in N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission
Rule 15A NCAC 03R .0117.
I. In accordance with Shellfish Temperature and Sanitation Controls Proclamation
SS-1-2026.
1. It is unlawful to possess oysters in a commercial fishing operation without a
harvest tag recording the time of the start of harvest, and information as
described in N.C. Marine Fisheries Commission Rule 15A NCAC 03K .0109,
affixed to each container of oysters. The time of the start of harvest shall be
the time when the first oyster is initially removed from the water.
2. It is unlawful for harvesters to fail to deliver oysters harvested from Coastal
Fishing Waters to a licensed shellfish dealer within 12 hours of the time of
the start of the harvest.
